Stories
from the field.

Doctoral research at the Centre of African Studies, University of Edinburgh, takes its students to refugee camps in Tanzania, water utilities in Chad, port construction sites in Kenya, and the offices of civil servants in Malawi. Here is what some of them found — and where their work has taken them since.
